Madli shows ranking gaps rather than hiding them; a close call is stated, never smoothed over.

```jsx
<RankGap tone="clear" points={11} comparedTo="#2" />
<RankGap tone="close" note="Nearly tied with #2 — either works" />
<RankGap tone="thin" note="Only 22 ratings — treat as a hint" />
```

Wording stays matter-of-fact: no "trust us", no exclamation marks.
