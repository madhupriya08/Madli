The reason is the product. It is never truncated, never collapsed behind a "more" link, and is capped at `--reason-max` (46ch) so it always sets in two or three lines.

```jsx
<ReasonNote>Locals rank it top for a weekday lunch: no queue before 1pm and the set menu is 180₺.</ReasonNote>
<ReasonNote tone="gem" label="Why this is a gem">Nine tables, no sign, and 84% of the people rating it live within a kilometre.</ReasonNote>
```
